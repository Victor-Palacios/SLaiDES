**T-020 — Harrington non-linear score combiner** (Phase 10). The aesthetic scorer now
offers an opt-in `harrington` composite mode — a **weighted geometric mean**
`100·Π subscore_i^(w_i/Σw)` (the Harrington desirability, ref #10, Harrington et al.,
DocEng 2004) — alongside the existing default weighted-arithmetic `mean`. Under the
geometric combiner one near-zero sub-score drives the whole slide composite toward 0,
so a single severe flaw (an overlap, an unreadable run) **dominates** instead of being
averaged away — exactly what the AESTHETICS.md design note (and the verified paper)
called for. This closes the last coded Phase-10 backlog item; the only remaining
Phase-10 card is **T-021** (weight calibration), which stays **DEFERRED** for want of a

## T-020 — Harrington combiner — commit `ad89adc`

- `_combine_mean` / `_combine_harrington` + a `COMBINERS` registry and `DEFAULT_COMBINE`
  in `slidekit/aesthetics/score.py`; `score_deck(deck, resolved, weights=, combine=)`
  selects the mode (raises `ValueError` on an unknown mode). Exported from the package.
- `harrington` = weighted geometric mean via logs: `100·exp(Σ w_i·ln(sub_i)/Σ w_i)`, with
  a `1e-9` floor so a 0.0 sub-score yields a near-zero (not NaN) composite while still
  expressing dominance. Exponents `w_i/Σw` sum to 1.
- **`mean` stays the default**, so every documented baseline and the designed-beats-plain
  invariant are unchanged. By AM-GM the geometric mean is **≤** the arithmetic mean for
  any fixed sub-score vector, with **equality iff all sub-scores are equal** — a uniformly
  good slide scores identically in both modes.
- **Sub-scores, advisory `W_AESTH_*` warnings, and the `deck = mean(slides)·consistency`
  roll-up are mode-independent** — only the per-slide composite changes (verified by test).
- CLI: `slidekit score deck.yaml --combine {mean,harrington}` (default `mean`); the
  plain-text header now labels the mode, e.g. `deck aesthetic score: 78.9/100 (harrington)`.
  Spot check on `examples/10_all_components.yaml`: mean **81.3** vs harrington **78.9**.
- Tests: **7 unit** (`tests/test_aesthetics/test_score.py`: uniform-equality, AM-GM
  inequality, one-flaw-dominates, weight-skews-exponent, score_deck determinism +
  ≤-mean + mode-independent sub-scores/warnings, unknown-mode rejection, registry) and
  **3 CLI** (`tests/test_cli/test_score_cli.py`: harrington runs + labels mode, ≤ mean,
  argparse rejects an unknown mode).
- Docs: `docs/AESTHETICS.md` composite design-note updated from "intended improvement"
  to **BUILT (T-020)** with the formula + AM-GM properties; `docs/RESEARCH_TRACE.md`
