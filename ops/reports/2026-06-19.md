## T-019 — info_density sub-score — commit `e8a4e2c`

- `_info_density(nodes, cw, ch)` averages two deterministic band scores via a new
  `_band(v, lo, hi, soft_lo, soft_hi)` helper:
  - **words-per-slide** (counted from wrapped line text, else `text_content`): full
    credit `[1, 45]`, ramping to 0 at 130 (crowding). Generous low end protects hero
    layouts.
  - **text coverage** (Σ text-rect area / canvas area): full credit `[0.02, 0.45]`,
    ramping to 0 at 0.85 (crowding) and from 0 at the low end (barrenness).
- A slide with no text node (e.g. `image-full-bleed`) earns full credit; a slide with
  no content nodes scores 0.0 (consistent with the other metrics).
- Wired into `score_deck` sub-scores; weight `1.0` in `DEFAULT_WEIGHTS`; advisory
  `W_AESTH_DENSITY` (threshold `0.45`) added to `ADVISORY_THRESHOLDS` / `_WARN_META`.
- **Designed-beats-plain invariant preserved** with density included:
  big-number **89.5** > agents-in-ai **84.4** > all-components **82.7**.
- 3 new unit tests (crowding penalised, sparse hero protected, image-only full credit /
  empty 0.0). `docs/AESTHETICS.md` + `docs/RESEARCH_TRACE.md` updated.
