## Designs completed — commit `53bf470`

Every shape is rendered with **measurable rects only**, per the Phase 9 determinism
rule (no freehand connectors; the linter still proves fit):

- **`process-steps`** — a horizontal numbered flow: N columns, each a numbered accent
  chip over a bold label (brand primary) and a body description. Chip + label + body
  share a `group_id` so the tight internal spacing is exempt from `E_GAP` (the
  numbered-steps pattern). No connector lines. Example `31` (4 steps).
- **`roadmap`** — a phased plan across vertical lanes: each phase is a column with an
  accent header band (title on top, surface text, grouped) over a bulleted item list.
  Example `32` (3 lanes).
- **`funnel`** — narrowing stages: a vertical stack of **centered accent bars**, each
  narrower than the one above (linear from full width to 0.45×), with the stage label
  and optional value (`label · value`) on top, grouped with its bar. The narrowing is
  the deterministic funnel read. Example `33` (4 stages).
- **`pyramid`** — a layered hierarchy: stacked bars **widening toward the base** (0.4×
  at the apex to full width), alternating brand-primary and accent fills, each layer's
  label on top. Example `34` (4 layers).
- **`matrix-2x2`** — quadrant positioning: a 2×2 field split by a centered accent
  **cross** (one vertical + one horizontal rule, sharing a `group_id` so their
  intersection is intentional and exempt from `E_OVERLAP`), each quadrant holding its
  label inset ≥0.3" from the cross, with the axis names as captions top and bottom.
  Example `35`.
- **`swot`** — a 2×2 of titled panels: Strengths/Opportunities headed in accent,
  Weaknesses/Threats in muted, each a heading over a bulleted list (reusing the
  `_panel_items` marker helper). No quadrant background fills (restraint over
  ornament). Example `36`.
