### Standing research — color_harmony tolerance grounded (commit `3a033cf`, board T-051)
The `color_harmony` row cited ref #19 (Cohen-Or et al., "Color Harmonization", SIGGRAPH
2006) for the *idea* of harmonic hue-wheel templates, but carried **no published angular
numbers**, and the 30° tolerance was flagged HEURISTIC with no characterization of how far
it sat from the paper. This was the next remaining HEURISTIC-constant lead.

- **Verified the exact template sector widths from the primary source.** Fetched the
  official PDF (igl.ethz.ch/projects/color-harmonization/harmonization.pdf) and extracted
  its **Appendix**: small sectors (types i, L, I, Y) = 5% of the disk = **18°**; large
  sectors (V, Y, X) = 26% = **93.6°**; the large sector of L = 22% = **79.2°**; type T =
  50% = **180°**; centre-to-centre separation of the two sectors is **180°** (I, X, Y) and
  **90°** (L).
- **Assessment vs slidekit's 30° tolerance.** `_color_harmony` rewards a hue-difference
  near {0, 30, 120, 150, 180}° with a linear falloff over a 30° window. The 30° falloff
  **lies within** the paper's 18°–93.6° sector range, and the 180° complementary reward
  matches the I/X/Y centre separation — so 30° is **consistent with** Cohen-Or's published
  geometry. BUT **30° is not a value the paper states** (its widths are 18/79.2/93.6/180°),
  and slidekit's 120°/150° (triadic/split-comp) reward angles trace to the broader artist
  colour-wheel tradition (Itten, cited only as *background* in Cohen-Or), not Cohen-Or's
  own template centre-separations (90°/180°).
- **Decision: 30° tolerance stays HEURISTIC** — but the trace moves from "templates exist
  at fixed angular relationships (no numbers)" to "exact widths VERIFIED; 30° shown
  consistent-with, not stated-by, the paper." **No metric behaviour change** (grounding,
  not recalibration). Did NOT tighten the metric toward Cohen-Or's 18° small-sector: that
  would change scores and belongs with the DEFERRED weight calibration, not an unprompted
  edit. Updated REFERENCES.md (#19 annotation + integration log), RESEARCH_TRACE.md
  (color_harmony row → "VERIFIED templates+exact widths / HEURISTIC tolerance"; scoreboard),
  AESTHETICS.md (lineage note), the `_color_harmony` code comment, NOTES.md, and the board
  (T-051 research, done). No new reference added — deepened an existing verified one.
